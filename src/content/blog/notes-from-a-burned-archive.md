The catalogue said forty-two boxes. The reading room produced three.

## What the fire chose

Fires are not random editors. They take the outer shelves first, the ones nearest the door, which in most municipal archives means the most recently deposited material. So the twentieth century burns and the eighteenth survives, and a generation of historians quietly concludes that the eighteenth century was better documented.

It was not. It was simply further from the door.

## Working with what is left

Three boxes is not nothing. Two of them held payroll ledgers, which are the least loved and most useful documents in any collection - names, dates, sums, repeated weekly for years. From a payroll you can reconstruct a workforce; from a workforce you can reconstruct a neighbourhood.

The third box held complaints. I spent four days with it and I am still thinking about it.
