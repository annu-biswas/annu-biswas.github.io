Nine years is a long time to spend on two hundred pages, and for most of them I believed I was failing.

## What the years were for

Four were archival - six countries, a lot of trains, one very patient partner. Two were spent on a chapter I eventually deleted. The remaining three were spent learning what the book was actually about, which turned out to be a different question than the one I had proposed.

## The case for slowness

Research that is rushed produces arguments that are borrowed. The long middle stretch, where nothing appears to be happening, is where the borrowed argument gets replaced by your own.
