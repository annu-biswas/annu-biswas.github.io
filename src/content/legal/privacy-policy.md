This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

## What is collected

Nothing is collected by this site directly. The contact form is currently a demonstration and does not transmit or store what you type into it.

## Hosting logs

GitHub Pages, like any web host, may record standard request information such as IP address and user agent for the purpose of serving and protecting the site. This is governed by GitHub's own privacy statement.

## External links

Pages on this site link out to publishers, booksellers, journals and video platforms. Once you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that service applies.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address listed on the contact page.
