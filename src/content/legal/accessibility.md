This site aims to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

## What has been done

Text and background combinations are checked for contrast. The site is navigable by keyboard alone, with visible focus indicators throughout. Headings follow a logical order, images carry alternative text, and the layout reflows to a single column on small screens without horizontal scrolling. Motion is reduced automatically for visitors whose system requests it.

## Known limitations

Some third-party content linked from this site - publisher pages, video platforms and PDF documents - is outside our control and may not meet the same standard.

## Reporting a problem

If you encounter a barrier on this site, please describe it via the contact page and it will be addressed.
